Changes for the Ak Bulut presentation:
- Kept the MAN cab, glazing, mirrors and grille from the scan.
- Cropped the original trailer and chassis; reduced the cab to 260,000 triangles, then removed the uneven scanned wheel surfaces.
- Converted millimetres to metres and oriented the cab for the scene.
- Retained original texture UVs; initial 6144 px atlas capped at 2048 px WebP for desktop and 1024 px JPEG for tablet in realism phase 1.
- Reused simplified CabNear/CabFar meshes at distance on desktop; retained the full scan for close inspection.
- Preserved smooth scan normals; applied three gentle Taubin smoothing passes.
- Recoloured bodywork white and cleaned glazing in the application shader.
- Replaced scanned front wheels with separately modelled, rotating wheel assemblies.
- Added an original articulated chassis and open curtain-side trailer, visible loads,
  wheels, air lines, rails, lights, underrun protection and forklift animation.
- Reused the adapted cab on the quarry hauler, with an original six-wheel chassis,
  hydraulic tipper body, hinged tailgate and visible limestone load.
